Quran Routine

Daily Wird – completing the Quran

Alongside learning the 10 ayat: continuous reading of the whole Quran. ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr was told: “Recite the Quran in a month,” then down to seven days, “and do not do it in less than that” (al-Bukhārī 5054, Muslim 1159). Set your pace under Settings.

How the Salaf recited the Quran

Only evidence with a checked source: the Quran, the two Ṣaḥīḥs, and reports graded by a scholar of ḥadīth. No personal opinion.

1 · Learning: knowledge and action together

كانوا يقترئون من رسول الله ﷺ عشر آيات، فلا يأخذون في العشر الأخرى حتى يعلموا ما في هذه من العلم والعمل، قالوا: فعلمنا العلم والعمل

Abū ʿAbd ar-Raḥmān as-Sulamī about the Companions who taught him the Quran (among them ʿUthmān and Ibn Masʿūd): they took ten ayat and did not move on until they had learned the knowledge and the action in them.

Aḥmad, aṭ-Ṭabarī (introduction to his Tafsīr), aṭ-Ṭaḥāwī (Mushkil al-Āthār 4/83) · Shuʿayb al-Arnaʾūṭ: chain strong. dorar.net

كَانَ خُلُقُهُ الْقُرْآنَ

ʿĀʾisha about the Prophet ﷺ: “His character was the Quran.” The goal of reading is acting on it.

Muslim 746

كِتَٰبٌ أَنزَلْنَٰهُ إِلَيْكَ مُبَٰرَكٌ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوٓا۟ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْأَلْبَٰبِ

“A blessed Book which We have revealed to you, that they might reflect upon its verses and that those of understanding would be reminded.” See also 47:24: “Then do they not reflect upon the Quran, or are there locks upon their hearts?”

Ṣād 38:29 · Muḥammad 47:24

2 · Reciting: slowly, ayah by ayah, responding

وَرَتِّلِ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ تَرْتِيلًا

“And recite the Quran with measured recitation.”

al-Muzzammil 73:4

Anas was asked about the recitation of the Prophet ﷺ. He said: “It was drawn out.” He recited the basmala, prolonging each word.

al-Bukhārī 5046

Umm Salama: he ﷺ would break up his recitation ayah by ayah: “al-ḥamdu lillāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn”, then stop; “ar-raḥmāni r-raḥīm”, then stop.

Abū Dāwūd 4001, at-Tirmidhī 2927 · al-Albānī: ṣaḥīḥ

A man told Ibn Masʿūd he had recited all of the Mufaṣṣal in one rakʿa. Ibn Masʿūd said: “Rushing like the rushing of poetry?” Hasty reading is censured.

al-Bukhārī 775, Muslim 822

Ḥudhayfa prayed at night with the Prophet ﷺ. He recited slowly. When he passed an ayah of glorification, he glorified Allah. When he passed a supplication, he asked. When he passed a mention of seeking refuge, he sought refuge.

Muslim 772

Ibn Masʿūd recited Sūrat an-Nisāʾ to the Prophet ﷺ. At ayah 41 he said: “Enough.” His eyes were overflowing with tears.

al-Bukhārī 5050, Muslim 800

3 · Amount: no completion in under three days

To ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr: “Recite the Quran in a month.” He asked for more, until: “Then recite it in seven, and do not do it in less than that.”

al-Bukhārī 5054, Muslim 1159

لَمْ يَفْقَهْ مَنْ قَرَأَ الْقُرْآنَ فِي أَقَلَّ مِنْ ثَلَاثٍ

“Whoever recites the Quran in less than three days has not understood it.”

Abū Dāwūd 1394, at-Tirmidhī 2949 · al-Albānī: ṣaḥīḥ

4 · Preserving: review and recite in prayer

“Keep refreshing this Quran. By the One in Whose hand is the soul of Muḥammad, it escapes faster than camels from their hobbles.”

al-Bukhārī 5033, Muslim 791

“The companion of the Quran is like the owner of hobbled camels: if he keeps watch over them, he holds them.” In Muslim: “If he stands and recites it night and day, he remembers it.”

al-Bukhārī 5031, Muslim 789

Jibrīl reviewed the Quran with the Prophet ﷺ every year in Ramaḍān, and twice in the year he died.

al-Bukhārī 4998

5 · Understanding: tafsīr on the way of the Salaf

Order of explanation: the Quran by the Quran, then by the Sunnah, then by the statements of the Companions, then of the Tābiʿūn, then by the Arabic language. Tafsīr by mere opinion without knowledge is forbidden.

Ibn Taymiyya, Muqaddima fī Uṣūl at-Tafsīr. Tafsīr works on this way: aṭ-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr, as-Saʿdī.

خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

“The best of you are those who learn the Quran and teach it.” The Companions learned from the Prophet ﷺ, the Tābiʿūn from the Companions. The Quran is learned from teachers, not alone.

al-Bukhārī 5027

Applying it today

  1. Learn: a small portion (e.g. 10 ayat). Understand it with as-Saʿdī, memorize it, act on one thing. Only then move on. → Learn tab
  2. Wird: read continuously, aim to complete the Quran in at most 30 days, never in under 3 days. Slowly, ayah by ayah. → Wird tab
  3. Respond: at praise of Allah say “subḥān Allāh”, at a supplication ask, at punishment seek refuge (Muslim 772).
  4. Review: every day the last portions, and recite what you learned in prayer, especially at night (Muslim 789).
  5. Tajwīd with a teacher: have your recitation checked by a qualified teacher (al-Bukhārī 5027).
  6. No personal interpretation: take the meaning from the tafsīr of Ahl as-Sunnah, ask the scholars (an-Naḥl 16:43).
Left out (weak or unchecked chain)
  • “Stop at its wonders, move the hearts with it …” (attributed to Ibn Masʿūd): Muṣannaf Ibn Abī Shayba 8973, chain very weak and broken (ʿĪsā is matrūk, ash-Shaʿbī did not hear from Ibn Masʿūd). Source
  • “ʿUmar learned al-Baqara in twelve years”: often quoted, chain not checked here.
كانوا إذا تعلّموا من النبي ﷺ عشر آيات لم يجاوزوها حتى يعلموا ما فيها من العلم والعمل، قالوا: فتعلّمنا القرآن والعلم والعمل جميعًا
Abū ʿAbd ar-Raḥmān as-Sulamī about the Companions (Ibn Masʿūd and others): they learned ten ayat and did not go beyond them until they had learned the knowledge and the action in them. So: read, understand, memorize, act. Then move on.
